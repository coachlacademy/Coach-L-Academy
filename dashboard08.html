🕊️ You Are Held in This Space — Always At Your Own Pace

This course walks alongside you through grief. Some weeks may feel very tender. You are always in control of your pace — take breaks whenever needed. Grief has no timeline and no right way. If at any point you feel overwhelmed or in crisis, please reach out: Crisis Line (📞 0800 12 13 14) 0800 567 567 Suicide & Crisis Lifeline. This course offers compassionate support and education — it complements but does not replace professional grief counselling or therapy.
