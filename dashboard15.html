🗣️ Communication Is a Skill — Not a Personality Trait

Whether you consider yourself naturally quiet or naturally talkative, introverted or extroverted — effective communication is something every person can learn and improve. This course covers the full spectrum: listening, speaking, non-verbal presence, assertiveness, and conflict resolution. Some of these skills may feel uncomfortable at first — particularly if past communication patterns have caused pain. Move through that discomfort with patience and self-compassion. The ability to connect, express, and resolve is one of the most transformative skills you can develop.
