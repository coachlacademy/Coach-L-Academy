Step 3
Where Do You Feel It In Your Body?
Anger lives in the body before it reaches the mouth. Recognising your physical signs is the first step to catching it early.

When you're triggered, your body activates before your mind catches up. Cortisol spikes. Heart rate climbs. Blood flows to your muscles. This is your body preparing to fight or flee — not a sign that you're broken. It's biology. But you can learn to catch it.

Step 4
How Did You Respond?
Honest reflection — not self-judgement. Understanding your response pattern is how you change it.
The 4 Anger Responses
🔥 Explode
Anger out — fast and loud. Short-term relief but often leaves damage. The words said cannot be unsaid.
🧊 Freeze / Shut Down
Anger in — you go cold, quiet, or numb. The anger doesn't disappear. It compounds until it forces its way out.
🌀 Displace
Anger sideways — it lands on someone safer. Your partner. Your kids. Your dog. Not the source of the anger.
✅ Process
Feel it → name it → pause → respond. Not suppress. Not explode. The goal: be honest without doing damage.
