Privacy Policy

Coach L Academy (“we”, “us”, or “our”) respects your privacy and is committed to protecting your personal information. We may collect the following types of information:

  • Name or display name (e.g. for live sessions, podcasts, or chat)

Your information is used only to:

Coach L Academy may use third-party services such as Jitsi for live audio and video sessions. Participation in live sessions is voluntary.

We do not record live sessions unless explicitly stated. Any recording, if applicable, will be communicated in advance.

4. Coaching Disclaimer

Coach L practices strictly as a Life Coach.

Coach L is not a psychologist, psychiatrist, medical professional, or social worker. The services provided through Coach L Academy are intended for personal development, motivation, education, and life guidance only.

Coach L Academy does not provide mental health diagnosis, therapy, or clinical treatment. Users experiencing mental health challenges are encouraged to seek assistance from qualified healthcare or mental health professionals.

5. Data Sharing

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal information. Data may only be shared with trusted service providers where necessary to operate the platform (e.g. hosting, streaming, or communication services).

6. Data Security

Reasonable technical and organizational measures are taken to protect your information. However, no method of transmission over the internet is 100% secure.

Coach L Academy is not directed at children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.
