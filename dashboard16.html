💕 A Note on Safety and Sensitivity

Relationships are where our deepest joys and deepest wounds live. This course explores what makes relationships healthy and what makes them harmful — including red flags, toxic patterns, and the lasting impact of difficult relationship experiences. Some content may bring up painful memories or current situations. Please move through it with self-compassion. If you are currently in a relationship that feels unsafe, support is available: National Domestic Violence Hotline: 1-800-799-7233 | Crisis Text Line: text HOME to 741741.
