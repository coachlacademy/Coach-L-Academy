Frequently Asked Questions

Can people tune in from anywhere in the world?

Yes. Your station streams globally. Anyone with internet access can listen or watch live.

Do I always have to be online to preach?

No. Sermons, shows, and podcasts can be scheduled in advance. Your station can run 24/7 automatically.

Is podcast included?

Yes. All sermons and shows can be converted into podcast episodes for on-demand listening.

How many studio guests can join?

Starter allows up to 5 studio guests. Foundation allows up to 10. Higher packages expand capacity further.

Can the host and guest see each other?

Yes. Professional two-way remote broadcasting allows host and guest to see each other during interviews.

Can our international audience see us live?

Yes. With website-enabled packages, your global audience can watch you live while broadcasting or DJing.

Are prayer sessions supported?

Yes. Integrated one-on-one and group video prayer sessions are included in higher tiers.
