🌿 A Note on Sensitivity and Support

Family relationships are often where our deepest wounds originate — and where our most profound healing can occur. This course explores difficult terrain: dysfunction, generational trauma, estrangement, and painful patterns that may have shaped your life in ways still unfolding. Move through this material with self-compassion and at your own pace. If you are in emotional distress, please reach out for support:

Crisis Line: 📞 0800 12 13 14  |  Suicide Helpline: 0800 567 567
