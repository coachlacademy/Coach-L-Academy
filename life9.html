Step 3
Recognising Spiritual Attacks
Not everything that comes against you is natural. Some attacks come through people — colleagues, friends, family — and some come directly at your mind and spirit. Name what you are facing.
⚔️ What Is a Spiritual Attack?

Spiritual warfare is real — described throughout Scripture as the battle between light and darkness, between the Kingdom of God and the forces that oppose it. Ephesians 6:12 says: "For we do not wrestle against flesh and blood, but against principalities, against powers, against the rulers of the darkness of this age."

This means that when people come against you — when colleagues sabotage, when family curses, when friends betray — the attack is not ultimately from that person. They are being used as a vessel. Understanding this is not about excusing the behaviour. It is about directing your battle to the right place — on your knees, in prayer, in the Spirit.
